# Scorecard (Dieter Rams)

1. **Innovative: 2/3**
   - Evidence: 9-scene MotionPath SVG pipeline.
   - Why: a clear improvement on the usual feature-grid landing page, but not a new pattern.
2. **Useful: 1/3**
   - Evidence: 24,107 px and 20 chapters to scroll.
   - Why: the core story (pipeline + zones) is spread across sections 01, 04, 05, 07 and 11, so readers take detours.
3. **Aesthetic: 1/3**
   - Evidence: 20 font sizes, 32 hex values.
   - Why: many inconsistencies; the micro-type (6–9 px) is a jarring violation.
4. **Understandable: 2/3**
   - Evidence: controls are clearly labelled.
   - Why: jargon like "backend-authoritative" needs a gloss.
5. **Unobtrusive: 2/3**
   - Evidence: dark chrome stays quiet.
   - Why: SVG decoration competes with the copy in a few sections.
6. **Honest: 1/3**
   - Evidence: 3 slot-level claims, the Zone A name conflict, the wrong "near full" flag.
   - Why: 2 or more inflations.
7. **Long-lasting: 2/3**
   - Evidence: restrained palette.
   - Why: one dated marker, the micro-label "spec sheet" trend type.
8. **Thorough: 1/3**
   - Evidence: no focus-visible styles, no skip link, 404 console errors, emoji icon.
   - Why: 2–3 states missing.
9. **Environmentally friendly: 2/3**
   - Evidence: 282 KB JS, reduced motion handled.
   - Why: under 500 KB with motion gated, but runtime Babel compilation is wasted work.
10. **As little design as possible: 1/3**
    - Evidence: the Demo, Highlights and repeated Architecture chapters could all be removed.
    - Why: 3–5 elements can go without breaking the task.

**Total: 15/30**
